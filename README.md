# Luso-Brazilian Legal AI Tools ⚖️🤖

An open-source initiative dedicated to using Large Language Models (LLMs) to solve complex cross-border administrative and legal issues between Brazil and Portugal. 

## The Problem
Vulnerable citizens, such as elderly pensioners, often face overwhelming bureaucratic walls when claiming their rights across borders. State agencies rely on complex statutes of limitations, outdated records, and lack of notifications to deny fundamental social security rights.

## The Solution
This repository aims to build and open-source a framework of specialized AI Prompts and Data Extraction Workflows designed to:
- Audit complex bank statements and foreign exchange operations (e.g., Brazilian Central Bank reports vs. European IBAN transfers).
- Cross-reference the Portuguese Administrative Procedure Code (CPA) with the Brazilian INSS regulations.
- Automate the drafting of high-level legal memorandums to combat illegal pension suspensions.

*Built for the community, to democratize access to international justice.*
